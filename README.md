## 🧭 Principles of the Craft

- Stay curious.
- Understand before abstracting.
- Build small things and finish them.
- Prefer useful solutions over impressive appearances.
- Learn from mistakes without turning them into judgments about yourself.
- Leave room for life outside the workshop.
